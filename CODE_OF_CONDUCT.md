# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:
- be respectful and welcoming;
- give and take feedback gracefully;
- assume good faith.

Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report anything that worries you to the maintainers at **info@med-technologies.com**, with "pairnexus-workflow conduct" in the subject. Reports are read only by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered as soon as we can. The maintainers decide what to do, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: from a private word, through a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban.
