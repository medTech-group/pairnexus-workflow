# Questions for the personas

Answer from your own side: as a customer if you are one, as staff (the owner or a stylist) if you are one. A question marked "(customers)" or "(staff)" is for that side only.

1. Walk us through the last time an appointment was booked, from the moment someone first thought "I need a haircut" to the moment it was settled. Who did what, how, and how long did it take?

2. (customers) How do you choose which stylist you see, and what happens when that person isn't free when you want to come in?
   (staff) How do customers end up with one stylist rather than another, and what part do you play in that?

3. (customers) What happens when you call or message the salon and nobody answers? What do you do next, and how often does that happen to you?
   (staff) What happens to a call or message that comes in while you are with a client? How do you find out about it, and what becomes of it?

4. When do you most often want to book, or find people wanting to book: what time of day, which days, and what is going on for you or for them at that moment?

5. What happens when someone needs to cancel or move an appointment today? Describe the last time it happened, from either side.

6. How do people get reminded of an appointment today, if at all, and what happens when a reminder doesn't come or is missed?

7. If someone had booked without signing up for anything, what would they need, and what would the salon need, to change or cancel that booking later? What would make that feel safe or unsafe to you?

8. (customers) What do you use to arrange things like appointments (a phone call, messages, a website, an app, in person) and on what device, and what makes you choose one over another?
   (staff) Where and how do you deal with bookings during a working day (at the front desk, on your own phone, between clients), and what does that cost you?

9. What do you need to know, or to tell the other side, for a booking to go well on the day: the service, how long it takes, the price, the stylist, anything else?

10. (staff) What do you need to see when a booking is made, and when and how do you need to see it, for it to fit into how you run the day?
    (customers) After you've booked, what do you need to hear back from the salon, and when, before you feel the appointment is really yours?

11. What has gone wrong with a booking in the past year, for you or for the salon, and what did it cost each side?

12. Suppose how booking works at the salon changed. A few months later, what would you look at, notice or count to decide whether it had made things better, worse or no different?
